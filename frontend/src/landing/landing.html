How it works

Three household signals, cross-checked.

Alerts compare against each resident’s own usual quiet gap, so outings stay silent and false alarms are cancelled before anyone is bothered.

01Primary trigger

Water

Silence beyond the resident’s usual quiet gap starts Unusual Inactivity.

When the routine breaks

A human checks in, and every step is recorded.

● Routine

On the day’s first activity, the caregiver gets a morning reassurance.

○ Possible Away

A door event, then no water or electricity activity. Silent: outings are left alone.

◆ Unusual Inactivity

Water silent beyond her usual gap. A grace period starts with a simulated voice call. Any activity cancels it.

▲ Unresolved Concern

Caregiver and township notified together, with reminders until someone taps “I’ll Check Now”.

“No water use for 0:34, longer than usual maximum of 0:30. No door event”

Every status comes with a plain-language reason line that names each signal behind it, so operators and families trust the alert.
