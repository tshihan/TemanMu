# TemanMu demo run sheet

## Before you start
- **Screen layout:**
  - **Left:** the 3D simulator (`/simulator`).
  - **Right:** two browser tabs, the caregiver app (`/caregiver`) and the township dashboard (`/dashboard`).
- **Make sure:** the caregiver tab is the one showing on the right, and nothing is still playing in the simulator.

## Part A: Setup (simulator)
1. Click **Reset demo**, then **OK** in the confirm box.
2. Explain the 3D home in one or two sentences:
   - It's only here for the demo, because no real home has sensors installed yet.
   - Clicking the tap or kettle sends the same data a real smart meter would. Her card taps at the lift lobby are the same taps the township's access-card system records.
   - Madam Lee's flat has no door sensor: her access card tells TemanMu when she goes out and comes back.

## Part B: Everyday days (watch the caregiver tab)
3. **Normal Morning:** press play, then point to the caregiver tab.
   - Point out that Sarah gets a calm "day has started" status.
   - Say this is fast-forwarded for the demo. In real life, TemanMu learns her routine from her own behaviour over a few weeks.
4. **Going Out:** press play, then point to the caregiver tab.
   - Point out the card tap at the lift lobby, and that Sarah sees "Mum went out" with the time only, not which gate.
   - Just say the caregiver is told she went out. Don't click or explain the buttons on this screen.
   - Mention again that it's fast-forwarded.

## Part C: The emergency path
5. **Unusual Inactivity:** press play.
   - Say this once, in one sentence: *"The quiet gap is the longest she normally goes without using water, learned by our Quiet Gap Model from her own history (1 h 55 m at 8 AM here, much shorter than overnight), and the grace period is a short extra wait after that before TemanMu calls her, so we don't raise false alarms."*
   - Optional: show **Mum's usual quiet gaps** on the caregiver home tab: short in the busy morning, long overnight.
   - TemanMu calls her and she doesn't answer.
   - On the **caregiver tab**, tap **Ask township to visit**.
   - Switch to the **township tab**.
6. **Township tab:**
   - Click **I'll Check Now**.
   - Wait until Guard Ali reaches Madam Lee in the 3D home.
   - Pick any outcome and click **Submit**.
   - Mention that **Call 999** is always one tap away on this screen.
7. **Close the incident**, then spend about 10 seconds on the township page:
   - One dashboard shows the whole block.
   - On the guard page, the guard sees the whole block and who needs attention next.
   - The guard never sees who's home, who's out, or their daily routine. Everything we demoed earlier only shows on the caregiver phone.
   - Only an alert brings a home forward.
   - Every alert ends with a recorded outcome.
8. Switch back to the **caregiver tab**.

## Part D: Access card (township tab)
9. **Not Back Yet:** press play, then switch to the **township tab** once the alert comes in.
   - She tapped out and hasn't come back. Point out the **"Not back yet · search outside"** label and the **last card tap** (place and time).
   - Say: the guard sees where she last tapped **only while an alert is open**. On a normal day the guard sees nothing about her outings.
   - Click **I'll Check Now**, then point out the steps: call her, check CCTV, search the township. Pick any outcome and **Submit**.
10. **Tapped In, Home Quiet:** press play.
    - She taps back in at the lobby but falls in the corridor before reaching her flat. Nothing is used at home, so TemanMu calls her and alerts both sides.
    - On the township tab, point out **"check the way home"**: the lift, stairs and corridor. Click **I'll Check Now**, pick any outcome and **Submit**.

## Part E: False alarm (caregiver tab)
11. **False Alarm Recovery:** press play.
   - She's napping, wakes up when TemanMu calls, and picks up the phone.
   - Point out that the concern resolves without anyone being sent.
   - **Expected behaviour:** the alert closes when its timer runs out, not the moment she answers. That's normal, so don't wait for it or comment on it.

## Part F: Companion (caregiver app)
12. Open the **Together** tab and explain it briefly:
    - **Nearby event:** a community activity matched to her hobbies.
    - **Community Buddy:** a volunteer who shares her hobby.
    - **Neighbour match:** someone nearby who keeps the same daily routine.

## Things to remember
- **Fast-forward:** Say it at least once. It speeds things up for the demo, but the real system learns from her actual behaviour.
- **If a scenario won't start** (for example "a previous incident is still active"), click **Reset demo** and start that step again.
- **Step 6 needs "I'll Check Now".** Guard Ali won't walk to her flat in the 3D home until it's clicked.
