# Slide 9: Care-Ready Home business case

Verified 10 November 2026. The headline establishes the family's need. Three large cards then present SDP's commercial opportunities, each with one concise benefit. The sandwich-generation explanation is reserved for narration. Four smaller statistics form a single supporting strip on presentation screens, including the added NHMS living-alone statistic. The proposed funding model is now a dedicated Appendix A1, removed from the slide 9 footer. Terracotta accents the opportunity numbers and statistics; the remaining content uses charcoal and cream. Commercial benefits remain hypotheses.

| Figure | Scope and source |
| --- | --- |
| RM16,667 per month | Estimated average household income published on SDP's City of Elmina commercial page. This is area-profile information, not a verified buyer income, disposable-income measure or willingness-to-pay result. |
| More than 11,000 homes | Completed homes reported on the same SDP page, which does not provide a date for the statistic. It is not the number of eligible older residents, paying customers or installations. |
| 8.4% | Share of Malaysia's population aged 65+ in DOSM's Current Population Estimates 2026. Do not relabel it as age 60+ or compare it directly with a differently defined threshold. |
| 18.8% living alone | NHMS 2025 infographic 3 (PDF page 6) compares 6.3% in 2018 with 18.8% in 2025 among older persons aged 60+. The ratio is 18.8 / 6.3 = 2.984, hence nearly threefold. The slide attributes this comparison to the 2025 infographic; the separate original 2018 factsheet reports 7.4%, so do not silently mix the two sources. |
| Approximately 770,000 people | Rough calculation: approximately 4.1 million people aged 60+ in infographic 1 (PDF page 4) × 18.8% = approximately 770,800, rounded to about 770,000. This is a calculation from rounded figures, not a separately reported weighted survey count. The slide labels it a rough estimate. |

Sources:

- [SDP City of Elmina](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/commercial/city-of-elmina).
- [DOSM Current Population Estimates 2026](https://www.dosm.gov.my/portal-main/release-content/current-population-estimates-2026).
- [MOH NHMS 2025 official infographic](https://iku.nih.gov.my/images/nhms-2025/infographic-eng.pdf), PDF pages 4 and 6, visually inspected.

The supplied 2030 projection uses the age-60+ definition and an older projection series. It is omitted. The slide labels the separate 2026 age-65+ population share and 2025 age-60+ living-alone prevalence explicitly; they are distinct measures, not a trend comparison. It makes no aged-nation classification claim.

The 32–48 buyer age range, market-wide competitor exclusivity, willingness to pay more, accelerated bookings and second-unit purchases are not established by these sources. The slide therefore presents differentiation, optional premium packages and multigenerational appeal as opportunities to evaluate. The sandwich-generation story is a proposed family need, not a measured profile of every SDP buyer.

Suggested narration:

> “These families are raising children while supporting ageing parents. Their parents want to remain independent, but their children cannot always be nearby and worry about who will notice if something happens. TemanMu could make that support part of a Care-Ready Home, giving families another reason to choose SDP. Elmina's household profile and housing scale, alongside Malaysia's ageing population, support exploring this opportunity. The pilot will test willingness to pay and purchase interest.”

Income, housing scale and national ageing establish context. They do not prove demand or a financial return. Appendix A1 proposes buyer-funded installation and ongoing service funded within an agreed maintenance package where appropriate. Costs, eligibility, approvals and payer arrangements need validation; individual-title homes without a shared arrangement need an alternative package.
